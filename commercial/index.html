Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines pour huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Each hour without climate control costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Lawrence HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Lawrence and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your records.

Preventive Care

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Lawrence HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand.

Next to an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
